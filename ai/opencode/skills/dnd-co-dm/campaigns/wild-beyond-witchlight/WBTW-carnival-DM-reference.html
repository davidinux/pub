2. Main Quest Location Flow

🎟️ Ticket Booth
Nikolas Midnight (elderly goblin)
Free admission (paid by Ellywick). Each PC gets butterfly wings, a carnival map, and a ticket with 8 punches.
Plot point: Nikolas ruses as half-blind — DC 13 Insight reveals he's sharp. Sends pixie messenger to Witch if PCs seem disruptive.
↓
★ Feasting Orchard
Ellywick Tumblestrum (gnome bard / Planeswalker)
Cake eating contest. Plot point: Ellywick is enigmatic — paid for tickets, can't be harmed, no magic reads her thoughts. Knows the incantation, the Hourglass Coven, and that the PCs are "agents of change." Fallback guide to Prismeer if they miss the incantation by Hour 8.
↓
🪞 Hall of Illusions
Candlefoot (mime), Rubin Sugarwood, Ween Sundapple
Mirror maze. Tasha's mannequin at entrance. Plot point: Portal to Prismeer through mirrors — needs incantation. Candlefoot lost his voice (corn husk doll → Kettlesteam). Rubin rescued from Sowpig → reunited with Ween → mood +1. Last PC to pass through hears "Rule of Three" + alicorn clue.
↓
🎪 Big Top
Mister Light, Mister Witch, Burly, Palasha, Candlefoot
Plot point: Midnight Extravaganza (Hour 4) and Dawn Crowning (Hour 8). Burly performs feats of strength. Palasha sings. Best opportunity for watch heist (W&L separated during show). Impress Light → private meeting.
↓
★ Staff Area / Witch & Light's Wagon
Mister Witch, Mister Light, Burly, Thaco
Plot point: Meeting W&L reveals the carnival's pact with the Hourglass Coven, Zybilna frozen, and the mirror portal. Thaco guards outside (untrustworthy per Burly). Watch heist — Witch's watch keeps the carnival running; without it, the carnival can't move between worlds.
↓
🪞 Hall of Illusions (Return)
Recite incantation before the mirror → portal opens to Hither (Prismeer). Watch and Vane cannot pass through. Balloon crash on the other side → arrival in Hither.

3. Useful but Skippable Locations

🦢 Gondola Swans
Feathereen (giant swan, speaks Common & Auran)
Hour-long gondola ride. Feathereen is erudite and haughty — asks metaphysical questions (e.g., "Does a dreamer know they dream?").
If impressed: Gossips about Candlefoot/Palasha romance, Burly's missing brother Hurly.
🪰 Dragonfly Rides
Northwind (treant sapling, CR2), Red (awakened squirrel, weasel stats, Int 10)
Northwind is HORRIBLE at keeping secrets. Spills: W&L are not original owners (swapped with Isolde). Carnival can't run without Witch's watch. Light loves smiling faces. Welcome Gifts happen here in Hour 1.
🎠 Carousel
Diana Cloppington (human-centaur, cursed by Skabatha)
Riddle of the Carousel: Paint 8 unicorn names correctly from proverbs. Reveals which hag has each PC's lost thing + hag lair location + hag weakness.
Diana is sad and bitter — can't speak about the coven (coughs sap if she tries).
🧚 Pixie Kingdom
Jeremy Plum, Honeymint, Starbug, Biscuit
Shrink potion, pixie dust. Jeremy assigns pixie names (encourages adoption). Hide-and-seek game. Rumors about the carnival's secrets.
🌊 Silversong Lake
Palasha (mermaid, CG)
Mermaid sings with control water effect. Summons quippers 1/day. In love with Candlefoot. Kettlesteam heckles her (3 jeers → Palasha flees). Offers singing lessons → useful in Ch.3 (Loomlurch).
🔍 Lost Property
Dirlagraun (displacer beast, speaks Elvish & Sylvan)
Old, gray-whiskered, carries toffee apple juice keg. Missing cub Star (followed Sowpig to Ch.3). Also Viro the lost boy. Suggests spying on W&L. Knows things go missing and never come back.
🐦 Kettlesteam Encounter
Kettlesteam (kenku warlock, CR1)
Grey-brown plumage, uses disguise self. Steals Candlefoot's voice → corn husk doll. Wanders carnival. Heckles Palasha at Silversong Lake. Knows Zybilna is frozen, overheard W&L arguing about the Hourglass Coven. Can't enter Prismeer (fey pact restriction).

7. Feywild Rules (Prismeer)

RuleDescription
Hospitality Once a guest accepts hospitality (food, drink, shelter), the host cannot harm them and the guest cannot harm the host. This is sacred in the Feywild — breaking it has dire consequences.
Ownership What is freely given cannot be taken back by force. What is stolen must be bargained for. This is why the hags use thieves to steal — they want "lost things" without violating fey law.
Reciprocity A gift demands a gift of equal or greater value in return. Be careful what the party accepts from fey creatures.
Zybilna (secretly Iggwilv / Tasha). Baba Yaga's adopted daughter. Frozen in time at the Palace of Heart's Desire. To free her the party needs: (1) an alicorn (unicorn horn), and (2) to speak her true name (Iggwilv / Tasha).
Last PC through the mirror hears: "Rule of Three… the alicorn holds the key."

8. DM Session Flow Checklist

  1. Hour 1 — Arrival
    Describe the carnival bursting out of nowhere. Northwind scatters golden seeds (Dex DC 10 to catch). Red hands out dandelions (write down wishes). Ticket Booth → Nikolas Midnight (DC 13 Insight to detect ruse). Butterfly wings + map + 8-punch tickets.
  2. Hour 1 — Welcome Gifts
    Roll d8 on Welcome Gifts table for each PC who caught a seed. Remind players of dandelion wishes.
  3. Hours 2-3 — Exploration
    Party chooses where to go. Prompt for Mystery Mine, Carousel, Hall of Illusions, Silversong Lake, Dragonfly Rides. Let them pick 2-3 locations per hour. Track mood changes.
  4. Check: Hall of Illusions / Rubin
    Sowpig encounter near the mirrors. Rubin rescue → mood +1 if reunited with Ween. Candlefoot's voice — did they get the corn husk doll from Kettlesteam?
  5. Check: Carousel Riddle
    8 unicorns, proverb-based names. Success → reveal hag assignments + weaknesses. Diana Cloppington's curse (coughs sap if she tries to warn).
  6. Check: Kettlesteam
    DC 13 Investigation to find (1 hour). Disguised. Mocking Palasha at Silversong Lake. Corn husk doll = Candlefoot's voice.
  7. Hour 4 — Midnight Extravaganza
    Mister Light ringmaster. Burly strength, Palasha song, Candlefoot mime. Offer PC participation (DC 14 Performance → mood +1). Group check → mood +3. Impress Light → private W&L meeting.
  8. Check: Watch Heist Opportunity
    Best time is Hour 4 (W&L separated). Burly proposes plan if befriended. Thaco guards Staff Area — Persuade/Deceive DC 12 or combat.
  9. Hours 5-7 — After-Show
    Final exploration. Gondola Swans (Feathereen gossip), Pixie Kingdom (hide-and-seek), Lost Property (Dirlagraun). Hag thieves lurk between wagons.
  10. Check: Return Voice to Candlefoot
    Untie corn husk doll → mood +2. Candlefoot proposes plan. Acting lessons → advantage in Ch.4.
  11. Check: Staff Area Infiltration
    W&L wagon. Confrontation or stealth. Learn about the pact, Zybilna frozen, and the incantation. Watch heist resolution.
  12. Hour 8 — Dawn Crowning
    Vane selects Witchlight Monarch. Crown of golden butterflies. Knighted by Light. Parade + fireworks. Charm of the Monarch granted.
  13. Check: Portal to Prismeer
    Recite incantation before mirror in Hall of Illusions. Watch and Vane cannot pass. Last PC hears "Rule of Three + alicorn." Balloon crash → Hither (Ch.2 begins).
  14. Fallback: Ellywick
    If party hasn't found incantation by now, Ellywick appears and gives it to them. "You were always going to find it. I'm just saving time."
  15. End Session
    Transition to Hither. Sir Talavar caged. Band of singing brigands. Clapperclaw or Agdon Longscarf as guide. Save point for next session.
Remember the tone: Whimsical fantasy with underlying dread. The carnival is joyful on the surface, but everything has a price. Every laugh echoes a little too long. Every smiling face hides a secret.
